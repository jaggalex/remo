## Result

The person gets access to the room with the chosen role. The access list shows where the role comes from: directly, through a team or through the organisation.

## Before you start

- You need the Share permission on the room. Only an owner or co-owner can change a team role and assign a co-owner.
- The person needs an account with the email you enter. If there is no account, an invitation link appears; send it yourself.

## Steps

1. Open Share on the room.
2. Enter the email, choose a role and click Add.
3. Check the source in the person's row: Directly, Through team … or Through organisation ….
4. To change a role, choose another one in the row. A team role can only be changed by an owner or co-owner.
5. To revoke access, click Remove in the row.

## Check the result

- The person can open the room after signing in; their resulting role is shown in Your role.
- If a person has several sources, all rows are visible and there is one resulting role: the strongest one.

## If it did not work

- **Share copies the link instead of opening the dialog.** The role cannot share: viewer, commenter, guest, or an editor when policy forbids it.
- **A team role does not change.** Only an owner or co-owner can do that.
- **The person cannot open the room.** Check that they signed in with the account you granted and that access was not revoked.
