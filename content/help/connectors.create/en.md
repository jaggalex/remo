## Result

Two objects are joined by a line. When at least one end is a shape, the line has an arrow.

## Before you start

- You need edit permission on the board.
- A connector can join sticky notes, text, shapes, images, stickers and cards. A frame, a kanban and another connector cannot: they have no anchors.

## Steps

1. Select an object. Four anchors appear around it, one at the middle of each side.
2. Click an anchor.
   - When the slot beside it is free, a connected copy of the object appears one gap away.
   - When another connectable object stands there, the two are joined with a line. The second end lands on the nearest anchor.
3. Or drag from the anchor to the second object and release on it. The result is the same: a line between the two objects.
4. If you change your mind, press `Esc` or click an empty spot: no connector is made.

## Check the result

- A line is visible between the objects; on a selected connector the ends are hollow circles, and every sufficiently long segment has a filled middle.
- A pair that already had a line did not get a second one.
- `⌘Z` / `Ctrl+Z` undoes the whole gesture: the line and the copy, when one appeared.

## If it did not work

- **No anchors.** The object cannot be connected: it is a frame, a kanban or another connector. A card connects through its cell.
- **No connector was made.** You may have landed on the same object: a self-connector is not allowed. Or the pair already has a line — a second one is not created.
- **A copy appeared instead of a line.** The slot was free; to join, point the anchor at an existing object or drag the line to it.
- **No arrow is visible.** An arrow appears when at least one end is a shape. A connector between sticky notes may have no arrow.
