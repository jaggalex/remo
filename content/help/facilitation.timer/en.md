## Result

One shared timer appears on the board. Everybody sees the same remaining time and who set it. A late joiner sees the current countdown, and reloading the page does not reset it.

## Who may control it

An editor or above controls the timer — the `board.facilitate` grant. A viewer or commenter sees the countdown but no controls.

## Steps

1. Open Facilitation ▾ and choose Timer.
2. Pick 1, 5, 10 or 15 minutes, or type your own.
3. Click Start. The timer appears in the middle of the current view and becomes selected.

While it runs, the Timer row in Facilitation ▾ shows the time left. Open the menu and click Timer to open the controls.

## Controls

- **Pause** keeps the exact remaining time and names who paused it.
- **Resume** continues from the same value.
- **+1 min** adds a minute; after "time is up" it starts a fresh one-minute countdown.
- **Reset** returns the timer to "not started" and keeps the chosen length.

## Check the result

- Open the board in a second tab: the countdown matches to the second.
- Pause: both tabs show one value and one name.
- Let it reach zero: the timer says "Time is up" and stays on the board until reset.

## Limits

- The timer is a board object: it travels into a backup and an export as a card with a time.
- There is no personal stopwatch in this version: the countdown on the board is always shared.
