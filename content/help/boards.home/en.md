## Result

You see the organisation home: board tiles with an emoji, title, team, author and dates.
The same screen carries name search, sorting, pages and the organisation switcher.

## Before you start

- You are signed in. Every account has a personal organisation with the **Personal**
  badge; it is fine for working alone or with an interest group.
- From a board, click **All boards** in the header or the organisation name left of the
  board title.

## Steps

1. Open **All boards**. Result: the address is `?org=…`, the board is closed, and the
   chosen organisation's tiles are shown.
2. Choose an organisation in the **Organisation** field. Result: the previous board and
   organisation are left behind and the list shows the new organisation's boards.
3. Search by board name. Result: the list narrows; an empty answer says nothing matches.
4. Choose a sort: **Updated**, **Created**, **Name** or **Author**. The arrow beside it
   changes direction. Result: tiles flow left to right, then top to bottom, in that order.
5. Page through 24 tiles at a time with the numbers and ‹ › arrows.
6. Click a tile to open the board. The tile menu changes the emoji, duplicates or trashes
   the board when you have the right.
7. Click **New board** to create one in the chosen organisation.
8. **Trash** beside **Boards** shows deleted boards; **Restore** puts a board back in the list. Only the owner can restore.

## Check

- The header names the organisation and shows the **Personal** badge for a personal one.
- A tile carries the title, team, author `Name @handle`, creation date and update date.
- The emoji survives a reload.
- A deleted board appears in the trash and restores back to the list.
- After switching organisation the previously open board is no longer shown.

## Limits

- The board icon is one emoji. Images and uploaded icons come later.
- The author is the creator; handing over ownership does not rewrite history.
- For older boards the creation date was restored from the last update and may be
  approximate.
- Search looks at the board name, not the text on the canvas.
