## Result

You see the same part of the room at the same zoom as the chosen person. Others can follow you the same way, and you can stop that.

## Steps

1. Click a participant's avatar in the header.
2. Your view moves to theirs; a pill appears: "Following …".
3. Click Stop on the pill to leave.

## Public counter

- Next to the avatars, the eye icon with a number shows how many people are following you right now.
- Click the counter to open the list, stop everyone, or bring everyone to you.

## Pause

If you pan or zoom yourself, following pauses: the pill says "Following paused" and offers Resume. The view never snaps back.

## Stopping from either side

- The **follower** clicks Stop.
- The **followed person** clicks "Stop everyone": the followers' pills disappear with a message that the follow was stopped.

## Bring everyone to me

"Bring everyone to me" is an invitation, not a jump: each person gets "Follow / Stay". If the group agreed in advance, an editor can turn on "Follow automatically", and the invitation moves viewports without a prompt.

## Limits

- Following is not saved: closing the tab ends it, and it never appears in a backup or an export.
- A public-link visitor sees no participants and cannot follow.
- The counter is public; hiding the fact of following from the followed person is not allowed.
