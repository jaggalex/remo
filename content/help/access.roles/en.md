## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including handing over ownership and deleting the room |
| Co-owner | Manages access and the link; cannot delete the room |
| Editor | Changes the room content |
| Commenter | Reads and comments, but does not change objects |
| Viewer | Reads only |

## Where a role comes from

A role can come directly, through a team or through the organisation. The access row shows the source; with several sources the strongest role wins. Revoking one source does not revoke access while another still works.

## Limits

- The room is the unit of access: permissions are checked on the room, not on a single object.
- A guest does not inherit organisation access: they get a personal room grant.
- A room in the trash does not open; only the owner can restore it.
