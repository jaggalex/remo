## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including handing over ownership and deleting the board |
| Co-owner | Manages access and the link; cannot delete the board |
| Editor | Changes the board content |
| Commenter | Reads and comments, but does not change objects |
| Viewer | Reads only |

## Where a role comes from

A role can come directly, through a team or through the organisation. The access row shows the source; with several sources the strongest role wins. Revoking one source does not revoke access while another still works.

## Limits

- The board is the unit of access: permissions are checked on the board, not on a single object.
- A guest does not inherit organisation access: they get a personal board grant.
- An archived board is read-only; only the owner can restore it.
