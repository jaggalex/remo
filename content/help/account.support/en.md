## Result

You wrote to support from the product and you know exactly what travelled with the message. If support is off on this installation, you know where to go instead.

## Where the button is

Support opens from four places:

1. **The account menu** — “Help and support”. The entry exists only when you are signed in and support is on for this installation.
2. **The help centre** — “Write to support” in the header of the help pages. The link is shown to a signed-in reader only: the help itself is public.
3. **A plan limit notice** — “Questions about your plan?”. It appears where the product refused because of a limit or a paid feature.
4. **A crash screen** — “Report a problem”. It appears when the app could not draw the room; there the technical information is already ticked.

No entry means support is off on this installation. The server administrator turns it on.

## What always travels

With a request, support receives what is needed to recognise you and your plan:

- the name and email address from your profile;
- the organisation’s name and its plan;
- the interface language, the app version and build, the installation’s region;
- a separate request number — it is not your account id and exists only for support.

This goes to the support server, not to remo’s servers.

## What travels with your consent

Above the chat there is a box, “Attach technical information”. It is unticked by default. When you tick it, the request gains:

- the app version and build, the browser and the operating system;
- the interface language and the window size;
- the room id and your role in it — without the room’s name;
- the last 20 client errors: the message and the time;
- the connection state the save line shows.

You can clear the box at any time: the request stays, the technical information no longer belongs to it. Error text is what makes “it does not save” and “a blank screen” answerable without asking you to open the browser console.

## What never travels

- Room content: cards, sticky notes, tables, comments, files.
- Names of rooms, organisations and members.
- Your password, codes from letters, card details. Support never asks for them: if a letter or a message asks, do not answer it.
- Other tabs and files on your computer.

If a room is needed to understand the problem, support will ask for a link or a fragment — the choice is yours.

## When a reply comes

The first line is an AI assistant: it answers at once and links the pages of this help centre and the guide. Money, account security, lost data, or an answer the assistant is unsure about go to a person.

A person replies during support working hours. The panel shows the exact wording, because the installation’s administrator sets it. The default is “A person will reply within a working day”. The reply arrives in the support chat and by email, so you can close the panel.

## If the chat does not open

- **A new tab opened instead of a panel.** On this installation support is a link to an external page or to a mailbox. Write there; there is no technical-information box in that mode.
- **The panel says support is unavailable.** The support server did not answer. Wait a minute: the conversation is not lost, it is stored on the support side.
- **The entry is missing from the menu.** Support is off, or you are not signed in.
- **The request number has to change.** An installation operator does that: the number is signed, and replacing it cuts off access to the old conversation in your name.
