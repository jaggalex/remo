## Result

You confirmed your email address or set a new password from a link in a letter. If mail is off on the installation, you know what to do instead.

## Confirm your address

1. Open the letter “Confirm your email for …” and click **Confirm email**.
2. The link works for 24 hours and once. You can open it in any browser, even without signing in.
3. Wait for “Email confirmed”. The request disappears from the account menu and the home screen.

Until the address is confirmed you can sign in and work in rooms. You cannot invite people unless the administrator turned that requirement off: the product asks you to confirm your email and offers to send another letter.

## If the letter did not arrive

1. Check the Spam folder and that the address in your profile is right.
2. In the account menu click **Send the letter again**. A new letter voids the previous link.
3. Wait: letters are sent at most three times an hour. If the product asks you to try later, try again in an hour.
4. If the letter still does not arrive, ask the server administrator to check the mail settings.

The home screen has a banner with the same button; you can hide it until the next sign-in. There is no banner in a room, so it does not get in the way.

## Forgot your password

1. In the sign-in form click **Forgot password?**. The link is absent when mail is off on the installation.
2. Enter the address and click **Send the link**. The answer is the same for any address: “If an account with this address exists, we sent a letter with a link. Check the Spam folder.”
3. Open the letter “Reset your … password” and follow the link within 60 minutes.
4. Set a new password. After the change every session ends, a notice goes to the address, and there is no automatic sign-in: sign in with the new password.
5. If the link has expired or was already used, request a new one.

## If mail is off

- There is no **Forgot password?** link. On a sign-in error the product says only the server administrator can reset the password.
- Address confirmation is not required, and no letters are sent.
- An invitation still creates a link you can copy and pass on by hand.

## Limits

- A letter carries a link, not a password. You choose the password.
- A reset link is single-use and works for 60 minutes; a confirmation link works for 24 hours.
- A new request voids the older link of the same purpose.
- A letter arrives in the profile language, or the browser language when the profile sets none.
