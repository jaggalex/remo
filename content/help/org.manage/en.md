## Result

You know how a personal organisation differs from a shared one, can rename the organisation, and run teams: create, rename, delete and add members.

## Personal and shared

- **Personal** — the organisation every account has. Work alone or with an interest group; the **Personal** badge marks it in the list.
- **Shared** — an additional organisation for work, a club or a client. Create it with **+ New organisation** under **Organisation and people**.
- Permissions and rooms work the same way; the kind only changes the badge and the fact that the personal organisation always stays with the account.

## Steps

1. Open the account menu → **Organisation and people**.
2. To rename the organisation, click **Rename**, enter the name and save. An organisation administrator can do this.
3. In the **Teams** block, create a team: enter a name and click **+ Create team**.
4. In a team row, rename or delete it. A team can be deleted only when no room or grant refers to it; otherwise the product shows the room and grant counts.
5. Expand the team members, add a person from the organisation and choose a role: team admin or member. Remove a member in the same place.

## Check

- The screen title shows the new organisation name.
- The renamed team appears in the list and in the team picker when creating a room.
- The deleted team is gone; rooms that referred to it were not allowed to be deleted.
- A newly added member appears in the team list.

## Limits

- Renaming the organisation and managing teams belongs to the organisation administrator.
- A personal organisation cannot be deleted: it stays with the account.
- A guest sees only their own seat and not the organisation's people directory.
