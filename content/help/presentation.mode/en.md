## Result

You present the board frame by frame: slides follow a chosen order, participants see the current frame, and when you stop the presenter returns to their previous view.

## Before you start

- The board has frames: only frames become slides.
- You are a facilitator (editor, co-owner or owner).
- The frame's size is the slide's size: if a section needs more room, resize the frame by a corner before presenting. The contents are not scaled.

## Steps

1. Click the ▶ icon (Start presentation) in the header. Result: a panel with the frame list.
2. Reorder frames with the arrows or click **Add all frames** if needed.
3. Click **Start presentation** in the panel. Result: the first slide appears with a counter.
4. Move with **‹** and **›**: the arrow keys pan the canvas, not the slides.
5. Participants see "{name} is showing the board" and click **Watch**; they join the current slide.
6. **Stop** closes the mode. The presenter returns to the previous view; followers stay on the last slide.

## Check

- A late participant starts at the current slide, not the first one.
- "Return to presenter" goes back to the current slide after panning away.
- After stopping, the presenter sees the same view as before the start.
- Without frames, the panel explains what to add.

## Limits

- Slides are frames only; arbitrary objects do not become slides.
- One presenter; followers cannot advance. If a presentation is already running, a second one cannot start: the product names the presenter and offers Watch.
- The presentation is not recorded and does not enter board history.
- A link visitor may watch but cannot start a presentation.
