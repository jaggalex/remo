## Result

Participants spread a limited number of votes across the chosen objects right on the board. The server counts votes and the quota, so reloading the page or opening a second tab does not give anyone extra votes. Voting stops by itself when the time is up — there is no voting "after the bell".

## Who may

- **Start a session** — an editor or above (`board.facilitate`).
- **Vote** — everyone who can see the board, including a public-link visitor. A visitor has a quota per browser and is never asked for a name.
- **End for all, extend and publish** — an editor only.

## Steps

1. Start the shared timer if the vote should follow a common clock: it then ends with the timer.
2. Select the objects to vote on.
3. Open Facilitation ▾ and choose Voting.
4. On the New session tab set the votes per person, the duration and anonymity. When the board timer runs, the "With the timer" option is available and shows the time left.
5. Click Start voting.

## How to vote

- You vote on the object: a counter with "+" appears in its bottom-right corner. A line above the board — "Voting is on" — shows the votes you have left, the time and who leads the session; the same line opens the panel.
- "+" casts a vote. When you have already voted for the object, the counter is highlighted, labelled "yours" and shows "−" to take the vote back. The number is always the sum for the object, not your share.
- When no votes are left, "+" is off and explains why. Voting happens on the board only: the panel has no voting buttons.
- Counters update for everyone at once; there is no need to move the board.

## What you see

- On the Manage tab, three numbers: time left, how many people voted and how many votes were cast.
- The session's objects with bars and totals; "Show on the board" selects an object and brings the camera to it.
- Totals per object — no names when the session is anonymous. With an anonymous session only totals are visible: nobody sees who voted for what, including the facilitator.

## Ending and results

- Voting closes by time automatically; **End now…** closes it earlier and asks for confirmation first.
- After it closes the voting buttons disappear, each object keeps its number marked "total", and the server refuses new votes.
- The Results tab names the leader, lists the places with bars for each share and states ties in words ("Places 2–3 shared by …").
- In an open session the results name people: an object's row opens with a button and shows who gave it votes, and the "Summary" block shows how much each person spent and across how many objects. Guests via the link are counted, not named.
- The "What participants will see" block shows the card that will appear on the board. **Publish on the board** writes it as a separate text object: a title, places and votes (up to 10), and the number of participants. It is ordinary text without formatting: lines like "1. … — N", with no bold or headings.
- Previous sessions stay under Previous sessions on the Results tab. An unpublished session can be published from there.

## If it did not work

- **"No votes left".** You used every vote; take one back with "−" on the object.
- **"Session closed".** The time is up or the facilitator ended it.
- **"A session is already open".** End the current one first.
- **An object has no counter.** It was not in the session's snapshot: objects added later do not take part.
