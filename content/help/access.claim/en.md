## Result

The room opens again. It gets an owner, joins an organisation and team, and the archive mark is cleared. After that, roles and the public link decide access.

## Before you start

- You have the room link. Without it the room cannot be found: it is not in the catalogue.
- You are signed in. If not, the button leads to sign-in; open the link again after signing in.
- Your account has an organisation and a team. A claim needs both.

## Steps

1. Open the room link. Result: the room is shown as closed, with a message that it has no owner.
2. Click **Claim this room**. If you are not signed in, the button reads **Sign in and claim it** and leads to sign-in.
3. Wait for the room to open. Result: you are the owner, and the room is in your organisation and team.

## Check

- The room opens from the link and shows its content.
- In **Share** your role is **Owner**; roles and the link are available.
- The room appears in the organisation catalogue.
- The public link turns on and off like any other room's.

## Limits

- While there is no owner the room is closed: the page refuses, the connection does not open, and the room is not in the catalogue. The link leads to the claim screen but does not open the room content.
- A claim needs an organisation and a team. Create them first in the account menu → **Organisation and people**; otherwise the server rejects the claim.
- Only a room without an owner can be claimed. If it already has an owner there is no button, and a repeated request is rejected.
- If the room cannot be claimed, the installation operator (whoever runs the remo server) can assign a known owner or archive the room. There is no automatic deletion.
