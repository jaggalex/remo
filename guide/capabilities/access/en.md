## What it gives

Managed access to a room: a role for a person, a team or the whole organisation, a public link with a password and an expiry, revocation in one step, and recovery of a room without an owner by claiming it.

## When to use

- Invite a colleague with edit permission.
- Show the room to an external person without an account.
- Close access after the project ends.
- Transfer the room to another owner.
- Recover a room without an owner when you have its link.

## How it works

A role is granted in the room and is combined from sources: a direct grant, a team, the organisation. The strongest role applies. The capabilities of the role define what a person can do: read, edit, share, export, make a backup, run facilitation, manage the room, transfer it, delete it.

A room without an owner is closed: roles and the public link give no access until it has an owner. A claim assigns an owner, puts the room in an organisation and a team, and clears the archive mark; after that, ordinary roles and the link apply.

## Example

Input: a room, a colleague without access. Action: **Share** → invite with the role **Editor**. Result: the colleague sees the room and can edit objects. A backup is available to them as an editor; PNG and PDF are available to the viewer role too.

## Recover a room without an owner

You need the room link and an account with an organisation and a team: the room is not in the room list.

### Steps

1. Open the link. Result: the page says the room has no owner and shows the claim button.
2. Sign in if you have not. If the button reads **Sign in and claim it**, it leads to sign-in; after that the **Claim this room** button becomes available.
3. Press **Claim this room**.

### Result

You are the owner, the room is in your organisation and team, the archive mark is cleared, and the tools are available.

### Check

- The message about the room having no owner is gone.
- The room appears in the organisation's room list.
- In **Share** you are listed as the owner and can enable the public link.

## What you can configure

- the role of each grant;
- enabling and disabling the link;
- the link password and its expiry.

## Limitations

- A room without an owner is closed: neither a role nor a public link gives access until it has an owner.
- A claim needs an account with an organisation and a team.
- A claim happens once: after that, the owner changes only by transferring ownership.
- A public link is view-only; export through it is forbidden.
- The **Editor** role can share the room only if the organisation policy allows it.
- A guest cannot manage access and does not inherit organisation access: the guest receives personal access to the room.
