## What is visible

Presence is a separate stream that does not change the document. It shows:

- who is in the room right now;
- where each person's cursor is;
- which reaction a person sent, if they sent one.

Presence is not saved: close the tab and you disappear from the list. The edit history stays.

## Name and colour

A participant is represented by a name and a colour. In a browser without a session the default name is a guest (`Guest 59a8`); a guest can set their own cursor name in the account menu, and it is stored in this browser. After signing in the cursor carries the account name and the presence tooltip shows `Name @handle`.

Every tab or device is a separate **session**. If one account has two sessions, both appear in the participant list: `Alexander Kostylev`, `Alexander Kostylev (2)`. Follow targets a specific session, so you can follow the right laptop or phone. A vote still belongs to the account: a second session does not get another quota.

Signing out restores the guest name. To test a two-person scenario inside one account, set a second name in the address: `/?room=<room>&user=Ann`.

## Follow

Follow is a subscription to another person's camera, not to their permissions:

1. you choose a participant and turn follow on;
2. your camera repeats their movements and zoom;
3. you can leave at any moment;
4. the person being followed can stop all followers at once.

Follow **does not give access**: if the room is closed, you will see only what you could open anyway.

## Calling everyone to you

This is an invitation, not coercion: a person receives a request, agrees — and ends up in your part of the room with follow on. A refusal changes nothing. If automatic follow is enabled in the room, the invitation does not ask — the view moves immediately.

## Practical consequence

Two people can look at different parts of the room and not get in each other's way. A shared picture appears only with follow or with an explicit jump via a link to an object.
