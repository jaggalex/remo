## Result

A new room is created in your organisation — in a team when one is chosen — and opened. It is empty or contains the chosen template, and its name is visible to every participant.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in and belong to an organisation as a member.
- The organisation has at least one team.
- You are not a guest: a guest cannot create rooms.

## Steps

1. Press **All rooms** in the header. Result: the organisation home with room tiles, search and sorting. If there are several organisations, choose one in the **Organisation** field: the list shows only its rooms.
2. Click **New room**. Result: a dialog with a name, organisation, team and template.
3. Enter a name. Result: it becomes the title of the new room.
4. If there are several organisations or teams, choose the right ones. Result: the room appears in the chosen organisation, and in the team when one is selected.
5. Choose **Empty room** or a template. Result: an empty room has no objects; a template adds its starting structure in the account language: Russian copy for a Russian profile, English copy for an English one.
6. Click **Create**. Result: the room opens and its id is in the address.

## Organisation and team

If you need a separate organisation, open the account menu under **Organisation and people** and click **+ New organisation**: enter a name and create it. Then choose it in the **Organisation** field on the home — that switches the context of the room list and creation.

A team is created in the same screen, in the **Teams** block; only an organisation admin can do this. There you can also rename a team, delete an unused one and add or remove members. A new organisation already has a **General** team; create another one for a separate direction.

## Check

- The title in the header matches the one you entered.
- The room is findable by its name on the home, in the **Rooms** tab.
- An empty room shows no objects; a template room shows the template's objects.
- The new room is in the **Rooms** tab on the home.
- The save indicator shows **saved**.

## Common mistakes

| Symptom | Cause |
|---|---|
| No **New room** button | you are a guest or not in an organisation |
| Creation is refused | you are a guest, the invitation is not accepted, or your membership is suspended |
| The organisation has no team | an administrator has not created one yet |
