## When you need it

Two or more people work on one board at the same time and need to see each other.

## Before you start

- The colleague has access to the board: a role is granted in **Поделиться** (see [Grant access and share a link](/guide/how-to/share)).
- Both of you have the board open in a browser.

## Steps

1. Check that the colleague appeared in the presence list in the header. Result: their name and cursor colour are visible.
2. Ask them to add a sticky note. Result: the sticky note appears for you without a reload.
3. Start following the colleague. Result: your camera repeats their movements.
4. Stop following with the button in the panel, or ask the colleague to stop their followers. Result: the camera stays where it is.
5. Disconnect the network for 10 seconds and add one more sticky note. Result: the indicator shows **офлайн (1)**, after the connection returns it shows **сохранено**, and the sticky note appears for the colleague.

## Check

- A colleague's changes are visible within a second.
- After the connection is restored the queue drains and the `офлайн (N)` counter disappears.
- Follow does not grant access: if the role is revoked, the tab switches to read-only.

## Common mistakes

| Symptom | Cause |
|---|---|
| The colleague does not appear | they have no access or have not opened the board |
| Follow "does not work" | you are looking at a different board |
| Edits are not visible | the connection is lost: check the save indicator |
