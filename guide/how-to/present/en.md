## When you need it

You need to run a meeting on the board: show frames in order, keep attention, and return to work afterwards.

## Prerequisites

- The board has frames.
- You hold the facilitation right: editor, co-owner or owner.

## Steps

1. Click the ▶ icon (**Start presentation**) in the header. Result: a panel with the frame list.
2. Reorder frames with the arrows; **Add all frames** restores all of them.
3. Start the presentation from the panel. Result: the first slide and a counter.
4. Move with **‹** and **›**: the arrow keys pan the canvas, not the slides.
5. Participants see "{name} is showing the board" and click **Watch**, joining the current slide even if they arrive late.
6. **Stop** closes the mode; the presenter returns to the previous view and followers stay on the last slide.

## Check

- A late participant sees the current slide.
- "Return to presenter" goes back to the slide after panning.
- After stopping, the presenter's view matches the starting one.
- Without frames, the panel explains what to do.

## Limits

- Only frames become slides.
- One presenter; followers cannot advance. If a presentation is already running, a second session or participant sees who is presenting and can only watch.
- The presentation is not recorded.
- A link visitor watches but cannot start.
