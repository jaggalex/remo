## What you can do with remo

Use remo to plan projects together, draw diagrams or organise tasks on a kanban board. You can create a room for each project. A room is a shared canvas where you can see your teammates’ changes.

Your role in a room determines what you can do with its content. With the Viewer role you can open the room. With the Editor role you can also change its content. The room owner can send a view-only link or save the work as a PNG or PDF. [Set up access](/guide/how-to/share)

## What you can do in a room

- **Make a plan.** Write ideas on sticky notes, connect them with lines and group related ideas. [Work with the canvas](/guide/capabilities/canvas)
- **Assign tasks.** Set assignees and dates on cards, then move the cards between kanban columns. [Cards](/guide/capabilities/cards)
- **Run a meeting.** Start a shared timer, collect votes or present selected objects as slides. [Timer and voting](/guide/capabilities/facilitation)
- **Discuss the work.** Leave a comment on an object or at a point on the canvas. [Comments](/guide/how-to/comment)

**Saved** at the top of the room means the server has confirmed that it stored your changes. [What happens when you lose your connection](/guide/concepts/sync)
