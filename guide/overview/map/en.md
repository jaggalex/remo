## Why this page exists

It answers "what can this product do at all" and shows which capabilities combine into working processes. If you need steps rather than an overview, start with [Grant access and share a link](/guide/how-to/share) or [reading routes](/guide/overview/routes).

## How it adds up to a process

A typical cycle: gather material on the canvas → structure it with frames and kanban → grant access to the team → take the result out as a file. When the work has to repeat, agents, events and the webhook join the cycle.
